At home

Not connected

Start the app to scan your router.

The scan is done by scanner.js. It looks only at your own router, from inside your network.

cd ~/Desktop/isp-visibility-checker && node app.js

Your VPN is on. Turn it off before you scan, or the scan stops or checks the wrong thing.

About these checks

Internet tests. They run in this browser, so they show this browser's traffic at this moment. They can't test a VPN's kill switch or other apps. Whoever runs your Wi-Fi sees the same things as your provider.

Router scan. It is done by scanner.js and needs the app started with node app.js. It looks only at your own router, from inside your network, with your VPN off. It never tests what the internet can reach, and it saves only sanitized-report.json and previous-sanitized-report.json.

Services the internet tests ask. ipquery.io, ipwho.is, geojs.io, Cloudflare, ipify.org, icanhazip.com, ip-api.com and ipleak.net, plus call servers at Cloudflare, Twilio, Google and Sipnet. Each one sees what any website would see. Nothing from these tests is saved.
